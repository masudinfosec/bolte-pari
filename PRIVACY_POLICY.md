# Privacy Policy — Bolte Pari

**Effective date:** 2026-08-29

Bolte Pari ("the app", "we", "us") is an offline English-learning application
created by an independent author. This Privacy Policy explains what information
is and is not handled when you use the app on Android, Windows, or Linux.

We designed Bolte Pari to work without accounts and without a server. Your
learning content and your progress stay on your own device. The only third-party
data collection in the app comes from Google AdMob, which is shown **only in the
Android version**.

---

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information ourselves.**

- The app has **no user accounts** — you never register, log in, or provide a
  name, email address, or phone number to use it.
- The app has **no server and no cloud storage of your data**. It does not
  upload your learning content, answers, scores, streaks, or progress anywhere.
- We do not run our own analytics, tracking, telemetry, or crash-reporting that
  identifies you.

All learning material (units, questions, explanations, model answers) ships
inside the app, and everything you generate by using it (progress, results,
streak, settings) is written only to local storage on your device.

The one exception is advertising on Android, described in the next section.

## Advertising & AdMob

The **Android version** of Bolte Pari displays ads through **Google AdMob**,
Google's mobile advertising service. The **Windows and Linux versions do not
contain ads** and do not include the AdMob component.

To serve ads, the AdMob SDK (operated by Google) may collect and process
standard advertising data, which can include:

- your device's **advertising identifier** (Android Advertising ID / App Set ID),
- your device's **IP address** and general device information (model, OS version,
  language, coarse region),
- **ad interaction data**, such as ads shown, taps, and diagnostics used to serve
  ads and prevent fraud.

This processing is performed by Google, not by us. We do not receive your
personal data from Google; we only receive aggregate, non-identifying reporting
about ad performance. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own
policies:

- **How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:**
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Google Privacy Policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **AdMob & AdSense data collection:**
  https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

### Consent for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

For users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and
Switzerland, the Android app uses **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to
present a consent form before personalized ads are used. Your choice on that form
determines whether Google may use your data for personalized advertising or only
for non-personalized (contextual) ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at
any time from within the app where the consent option is offered.

## Children's Privacy

Bolte Pari is intended for users **aged 13 and older**. It is not directed to
children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
children under 13. Because the Android version shows AdMob ads, it is not
designed or configured as a "made for families" / child-directed app. If you are
a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the app, you can
reset or clear the device advertising identifier in the device settings and
uninstall the app to stop all data processing.

## Data Storage

- All app content and all of your progress data are stored **locally on your
  device**. Nothing is synced or backed up to us.
- Because your data lives on the device, **uninstalling the app removes your
  local progress** (unless it was captured by a separate device- or OS-level
  backup that you enabled yourself, which is outside the app's control).
- We have no ability to access, retrieve, restore, or delete your on-device data
  remotely, because we never receive it.

## Your Choices

- **Ads (Android):** You can limit ad tracking by resetting or deleting your
  Advertising ID in **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads** (menu names vary by
  device). In the EEA/UK/Switzerland you can also change your consent choice
  through the in-app consent option.
- **Ad-free desktop:** The Windows and Linux versions contain no ads and no
  AdMob component at all.
- **Your progress:** You can clear your progress by clearing the app's local
  data in your device/OS settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to This Policy

document and publish the revised version in the project repository. Continued use
of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
